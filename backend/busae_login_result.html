Multi-fee Count
LEFT:
At the left stops, we see the passenger entries made at each stop in green. These are the entry counts. People who have boarded between the last detected stop and the next one are assigned to a stop. That is, if someone gets on 300 meters outside of the first stop, they will still be assigned as an entry at the previous stop; even if they get on 500 meters before the next stop. (Same pricing policy as the Passenger App when selling tickets)
TOP:
At the top stops, we see the automatic FIFO (First in First Out) assignment we make of the people who have boarded, based on the exit data detected by the counter. Here the process is absolutely inverse, that is, if a person gets off when leaving the geofence of the stop, it is considered that they have got off within the range of the second and so on until just detecting the next stop.
DATA TABLE:
In the FIFO assignment, we assign all the boardings, even though there may be a small difference between exits and entries in count reports. If there are more boardings than exits, the last boardings will be assigned to the last destination stop. If there are more exits than boardings, only the boardings will be assigned, leaving the excess of exits unassigned.
Multi-fee Count
In this report, we present data pertaining to the service carried out from the Busae application on the bus, calculating passenger entries and exits. The calculation is made ONLY between the start period of the journey in the app (possible ticket sales) until the end of the same journey upon reaching the last stop.
If there are passengers who get on before the trip begins with the App, their fare will not be calculated, the same if the app is closed before all passengers get off at the last stop. It is the only way to know exactly which journey each count corresponds to.
Stop Type

Types of Stops

  • Normal Stop: Assigned to routes or bus lines. Round trip, interurban, etc.
  • Yard or Garage: Location where buses stay overnight. They depart and return from their operation, etc.
  • Recharge Point (Recaudo): If your company uses recaudo, mark locations where NFC cards can be recharged.
  • Compensation Terminal (Counting): Stops where routes begin, with many passengers boarding and alighting. The counter takes the net (boardings minus alightings).
  • Rest Stop (Counting): Stops where passengers often get off to rest (long routes). The counter only counts boardings if there are more than before arriving at this stop.
  • Geofence / Not a Stop: To control crossing points of administrative vehicles, not buses.
